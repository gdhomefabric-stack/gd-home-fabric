# Launch checklist (owner, in order)

1. **Move the domain:** GitHub → `GD-HOME-WEBSITE-MAIN-` → Settings → Pages → clear `gdhomefabric.in` → Save; then `gd-home-fabric` → Settings → Pages → Custom domain `gdhomefabric.in` → Save → tick **Enforce HTTPS** when the DNS check is green.
2. **Send the four contact details** (WhatsApp number, email, postal address, Amazon storefront URL); they go into the `CONTACT` block at the top of `lib/content.ts` and stay hidden on the site until filled.
3. **Confirm the prices** on the site (Amazon.in, 26 Oct 2026: ₹1,799 / ₹3,500 / ₹3,799 / ₹2,199 / ₹4,300 / cover ₹100) and say when any price changes.
4. **Check the live site** at https://gdhomefabric.in on phone and laptop. Every push to `main` redeploys it automatically in about two minutes.
5. **Send real photos** of the pillow, the vacuum pack and the cover to replace the stock photography over time.
